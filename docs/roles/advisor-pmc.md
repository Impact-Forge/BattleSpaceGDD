# Loadout Options

Advisors can select from multiple specialized loadouts, making them versatile force multipliers:

**Standard PMC Loadout:**
- **Primary Weapon:** Premium assault rifle with advanced optics
- **Ammunition:** 8 magazines (240 rounds total)
- **Equipment:** 2x Fragmentation Grenades, 2x Smoke Grenades
- **Special:** Extra armor/plate carrier, advanced communications
- **Bandages:** 4x Field Dressings

**Auto-Rifleman Variant:**
- **Primary Weapon:** Light Machine Gun (premium models)
- **Ammunition:** 600 rounds (3 belts/drums)
- **Equipment:** 2x Smoke Grenades, bipod
- **Bandages:** 2x Field Dressings

**Grenadier Variant:**
- **Primary Weapon:** Assault rifle with 40mm launcher
- **Rifle Ammunition:** 7 magazines
- **40mm Grenades:** 10 rounds (mix HE/Smoke)
- **Equipment:** 2x Fragmentation Grenades
- **Bandages:** 3x Field Dressings

**Special Weapons Variant:**
- **Primary Weapon:** Carbine/SMG
- **Ammunition:** 6 magazines
- **Launcher:** Advanced AT system (Javelin, SMAW, etc.)
- **AT Ammunition:** 2-3 rockets
- **Equipment:** 1x Smoke Grenade
- **Bandages:** 2x Field Dressings

# Special Abilities

PMC Advisors are elite contractors with top-tier equipment and training, acting as force multipliers for their units:

**Force Multiplier:**
- **Loadout Flexibility:** Choose specialized role based on squad needs
  - Fill gaps in squad composition
  - Adapt to mission requirements
  - Provide extra firepower where needed
- **Premium Equipment:** Access to higher-quality weapons and gear
  - Better optics and attachments
  - Improved armor protection
  - Advanced communications equipment

**Veteran Training:**
- **Former Military:** Elite training and combat experience
- **Tactical Expertise:** Enhanced battlefield awareness
- **Multi-Role Capable:** Can fulfill multiple squad roles effectively
- **Independent Operations:** Can operate with reduced support

**Contract Advantages:**
- **Better Equipped:** Superior weapons and protection than standard roles
- **Flexible Deployment:** Not bound by strict military hierarchy
- **Specialized Skills:** Access to advanced techniques and equipment

# Advantages

- **Loadout Versatility:** Choose from multiple specialized roles per spawn
- **Premium Gear:** Better weapons, optics, and equipment than standard troops
- **Force Multiplier:** Add extra specialist to squad beyond normal limits
- **Enhanced Protection:** Better armor/plate carrier than standard infantry
- **Flexible Capability:** Adapt to changing battlefield needs
- **Combat Effectiveness:** Top-tier training and equipment
- **Squad Enhancement:** Provide critical capability boost to any squad

# Disadvantages

- **Loadout-Dependent Penalties:**
  - **Weight varies drastically** based on selected loadout
  - Auto-Rifleman: 20% slower movement, 30% slower sprint
  - Special Weapons: 25-30% slower movement, extreme stamina penalty
  - Grenadier: 10-15% slower movement
  - Standard PMC: 5% slower than Rifleman (due to extra armor)
- **Weapon Handling Penalties:**
  - Heavy weapons: Increased ADS time (15-40% slower)
  - Poor hip-fire accuracy on specialist weapons
  - Longer reload times on belt-fed weapons
- **Reduced Ammunition:**
  - **Less ammo than standard roles** for same weapon class
  - Auto-Rifleman: 600 vs 800 rounds
  - Grenadier: 10 vs 12 grenades
  - Special Weapons: Same severe limitations (2-3 rockets)
- **High-Value Target:**
  - Premium equipment makes you identifiable
  - Enemy prioritizes killing PMCs
  - Loss is significant blow to squad capability
- **Resource Intensive:**
  - May require team resources to deploy
  - Limited availability based on team economy
  - Expensive loss if killed
- **Role Confusion:**
  - Squad may not know which specialist you're filling
  - Must communicate loadout to Squad Leader
  - Flexibility can cause coordination issues
- **Multitasking Demand:**
  - Must understand multiple specialist roles deeply
  - Switching roles requires relearning tactics
  - Higher skill requirement than single-role specialists
- **Limited Availability:**
  - May be restricted to 1-2 per team or squad
  - Not always available to select
  - Competition for limited PMC slots
- **Armor Weight Trade-off:**
  - Extra armor provides protection but adds weight
  - Even standard PMC loadout slower than Rifleman
  - Protection vs mobility balance
- **Jack-of-All-Trades Risk:**
  - May not be as effective as dedicated specialists
  - Less ammunition than standard role equivalents
  - Versatility comes at cost of pure specialization
