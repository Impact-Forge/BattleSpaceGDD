# Character Description

A combat engineer is a type of soldier who performs military engineering tasks in support of land forces combat operations. Combat engineers perform a variety of military engineering, and mine warfare tasks as well as construction and demolition duties in and out of combat zones.

# Loadout

- **Primary Weapon:** Assault rifle or carbine
- **Ammunition:** 7 magazines (210 rounds total)
- **Explosives:**
  - 3x C4/TNT charges (for demolition)
  - 2x Anti-Tank Mines
  - 2x Anti-Personnel Mines (or Claymores)
- **Equipment:** 1x Smoke Grenade, Engineer Tool Kit
- **Special Tools:**
  - Entrenching tool/shovel
  - Wire cutters
  - Lock pick set for vehicle capture
  - Repair kit (basic)
- **Bandages:** 3x Field Dressings

# Squad Limitations

- **Maximum 1-2 per squad** - Specialized construction and demolition role
- Critical for establishing forward infrastructure and defensive positions

# Special Abilities

Combat Engineers are force multipliers who shape the battlefield through construction, demolition, and obstacle creation:

**Demolition:**
- **Structure Destruction:** Destroy buildings, bridges, and enemy fortifications with C4/TNT
- **Vehicle Destruction:** Plant charges on disabled enemy vehicles
- **Obstacle Clearance:** Clear enemy mines and wire obstacles (with kit)
- **Breach Creation:** Blow holes in walls for squad entry points

**Mine Warfare:**
- **Anti-Tank Mining:** Deploy AT mines to deny armor routes (max 2 placed)
- **Anti-Personnel/Claymore:** Set defensive traps and ambush positions (max 2 placed)
- **Mine Detection:** Locate and mark enemy mines for squad
- **Explosive Ambush:** Set ambushes on key terrain and supply routes

**Construction & Fortification:**
- **FOB Construction:** Build Forward Operating Base structures
  - Requires construction vehicle nearby
  - Build: bunkers, walls, towers, supply depots
  - Build time: 60-120 seconds depending on structure
- **Defensive Positions:** Create sandbag positions, wire obstacles
- **Infrastructure Setup:** Establish crucial facilities in captured territory
  - Build spawn points and respawn structures
  - Setup ammo/supply caches
  - Establish repair stations

**Vehicle Operations:**
- **Vehicle Capture:** Use lock pick set to access non-destroyed enemy vehicles
  - Captured vehicles can be driven but not rearmed
  - Can return to Motor Pool for resource income credit
  - Takes 30 seconds to pick lock (vulnerable during action)
- **Basic Repair:** Limited vehicle and structure repair capability

# Advantages

- **Battlefield Shaping:** Only role that can build defensive structures
- **Demolition Master:** Can destroy enemy fortifications and create breaches
- **Mine Warfare:** Deny terrain to enemy armor and infantry
- **FOB Construction:** Critical for establishing forward spawn points and supply
- **Vehicle Capture:** Can steal enemy vehicles for resources or use
- **Versatile Impact:** Construction, demolition, and combat capabilities
- **Obstacle Creation:** Shape enemy movement with mines and fortifications

# Disadvantages

- **Limited Explosives:**
  - Only 3 demolition charges and 4 mines total
  - Cannot resupply explosives from squad - must return to FOB
  - Must carefully plan explosive use
- **Weight Penalty:**
  - 15% slower movement than Rifleman
  - Explosives, tools, and equipment are heavy
  - Reduced stamina recovery
- **Time Intensive:**
  - Construction takes 60-120 seconds (very vulnerable)
  - Must stay near construction vehicle for FOB building
  - Vehicle lock picking takes 30 seconds exposed
  - Requires significant time commitment away from combat
- **Multitasking Demand:**
  - Must balance combat, construction, and mine placement
  - Responsible for establishing infrastructure in captured zones
  - High cognitive load managing multiple tasks
- **Construction Dependency:**
  - Needs construction vehicle nearby for major building projects
  - Cannot build without proper equipment
- **Vulnerability During Tasks:**
  - Completely exposed while building (long animations)
  - Cannot fight while using tools
  - Easy target when focused on construction/demolition
- **Limited Combat Focus:**
  - Slightly less ammunition than standard Rifleman
  - Time spent building means less time fighting
- **Mine Limitations:**
  - Maximum 2 AT and 2 AP mines placed at once
  - Mines can be detected and cleared by enemy engineers
- **Team Dependency:** Infrastructure effectiveness depends on team using it properly
- **Reduced Grenades:** Only 1 smoke, no frags (explosives take that space)
