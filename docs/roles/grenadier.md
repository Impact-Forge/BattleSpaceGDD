# Character Description

Armed with a grenade launcher, a weapon that fires a specially-designed large-caliber projectile, often with an explosive, smoke or gas warhead. These soldiers operate as part of a fireteam.

# Loadout

- **Primary Weapon:** Assault rifle with underslung 40mm grenade launcher (M203, GP-25, etc.) OR standalone launcher (M32, M79)
- **Rifle Ammunition:** 7 magazines (210 rounds total)
- **40mm Grenades:** 8-12 rounds depending on launcher type
  - High-Explosive (HE) rounds: 6-8
  - Smoke rounds: 2-3
  - Optional: Illumination or signaling rounds: 1-2
- **Equipment:** 2x Fragmentation Grenades, 1x Smoke Grenade
- **Bandages:** 3x Field Dressings

# Squad Limitations

- **Maximum 1-2 per squad** - Specialized fire support role
- Critical for engaging fortified positions and defilade targets

# Special Abilities

The Grenadier provides explosive firepower and can engage targets behind cover, in buildings, or at extended ranges:

**40mm Grenade Systems:**
- **Underslung Launcher (M203/GP-25):**
  - Attached to rifle, maintains rifle capability
  - 8-10 rounds of 40mm ammunition carried
  - Effective range: 150-400m
  - Reload time: 4-5 seconds per round

- **Standalone Launcher (M32/M79):**
  - Dedicated grenade launcher (6-shot revolver or single-shot)
  - 10-12 rounds of 40mm ammunition carried
  - Effective range: up to 800m for some platforms
  - Faster reload for M32 (revolver), slower for M79 (break-action)

**Engagement Capabilities:**
- **Defilade Targets:** Hit enemies behind cover with arcing fire
- **Building Clearance:** Engage windows and fortified positions
- **Vehicle Harassment:** Suppress or damage light vehicles
- **Area Denial:** Lay smoke screens for squad movement
- **Extended Range:** Engage targets beyond effective rifle range (up to 800m)

**Ammunition Types:**
- **HE (High-Explosive):** Anti-personnel and light material, 5m casualty radius
- **Smoke:** Screening and signaling, 30-second duration
- **Illumination:** Battlefield lighting for night operations (if available)

# Advantages

- **Indirect Fire:** Can engage targets behind cover without line of sight
- **Building Assault:** Clear fortified structures and windows
- **Extended Range:** 800m maximum range exceeds standard infantry weapons
- **Versatile Ammunition:** HE, smoke, and illumination options
- **Force Multiplier:** Single grenadier equals firepower of multiple riflemen
- **Tactical Flexibility:** Underslung maintains rifle capability

# Disadvantages

- **Limited Ammunition:**
  - Only 8-12 grenades total, must manage carefully
  - Cannot resupply from standard Riflemen - needs supply point
  - Each shot must count due to scarcity
- **Weight Penalty:**
  - 10-15% slower movement than Rifleman
  - Grenade ammunition is heavy and bulky
  - Slightly slower stamina recovery
- **Weapon Handling:**
  - Underslung launcher adds weight, increasing ADS time by 15%
  - Standalone launcher requires weapon switch from sidearm for close combat
  - Reload time is slow (4-5 seconds exposed)
- **Minimum Arming Distance:**
  - 40mm rounds don't arm until 15-30m from launcher
  - Ineffective in close quarters (dud rounds)
  - Must maintain distance from targets
- **Arc Fire Difficulty:**
  - Requires skill to judge trajectory at range
  - Need to account for target distance and elevation
  - Easy to miss with indirect fire
- **Resupply Dependency:** Must return to FOB/supply point to restock grenades
- **Reduced Rifle Ammo:** Carry less rifle ammunition than standard Rifleman
