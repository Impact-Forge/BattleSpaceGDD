As a Combat Medic, you'll administer emergency medical care in the field in combat, as well as primary care and health protection and evacuation from a point of injury.

# Loadout

- **Primary Weapon:** Standard issue assault rifle or carbine
- **Ammunition:** 6 magazines (180 rounds total) - reduced due to medical equipment
- **Equipment:** 1x Smoke Grenade (no frags - focus on saving lives)
- **Medical Supplies:**
  - 8x Field Dressings (for wounded soldiers)
  - 6x Morphine/Epinephrine (for revives)
  - 2x Saline Bags (for bleeding stabilization)
  - Medical Kit for treatment

# Squad Limitations

- **Maximum 2 per squad** - Critical support role
- High-value asset that must be protected

# Special Abilities

Combat Medics are force multipliers who keep squads in the fight. Your primary mission is saving lives and maintaining squad combat effectiveness:

**Medical Treatment:**
- **Field Healing:** Restore wounded soldiers to 85% health in field conditions
- **Revive Incapacitated:** Can revive downed players within 2-minute bleed-out window
  - Cannot revive headshot kills (instant death)
  - Revive time: 8 seconds exposed while treating
  - Revived players return at 50% health
- **Stop Bleeding:** Stabilize critically wounded to prevent bleed-out
- **Triage Priority:** Assess multiple casualties and prioritize treatment

**Medical Facilities:**
- **Forward MASH Deployment:** Can build Mobile Army Surgical Hospital near medical vehicle
  - Allows full heal to 100% health
  - Provides respawn point for critically wounded
  - Takes 90 seconds to construct
  - Requires medical supply vehicle within 25m
- **Resupply Station:** MASH acts as medical resupply for other medics

# Advantages

- **Save Lives:** Only role that can revive incapacitated teammates
- **Sustain Combat Power:** Keep squad fighting at full strength
- **Field Healing:** Restore soldiers to 85% without need for vehicle/base
- **Multiple Patients:** Can treat many wounded before resupply needed (8 dressings, 6 revives)
- **Force Multiplier:** Each save prevents ticket loss and maintains squad cohesion
- **MASH Capability:** Create forward medical facility for full healing

# Disadvantages

- **Reduced Combat Capability:**
  - Less ammunition than Rifleman (6 vs 8-10 mags)
  - No fragmentation grenades - limited offensive capability
  - Medical equipment adds weight penalty
- **High-Risk Exposure:**
  - Must expose yourself to save wounded (8-second revive animation)
  - Reviving in open areas is extremely dangerous
  - Priority target for enemies who understand your value
- **Limited Field Healing:** Can only restore to 85% max without MASH/vehicle
- **Resource Dependent:**
  - Limited supplies - only 8 bandages and 6 revive doses
  - Must return to supply point or MASH to restock
  - MASH requires proximity to medical vehicle
- **Headshot Limitation:** Cannot revive headshot casualties
- **Bleed-Out Timer:** Must reach wounded within 2-minute window
- **Multitasking Demand:** Must balance combat, situational awareness, and medical duties
- **Squad Dependent:** Effectiveness relies on squad protecting you while you work
- **No 100% Field Heal:** Cannot fully heal soldiers in combat without MASH setup
