# Loadout

**Anti-Tank (AT) Variant:**
- **Primary Weapon:** Carbine or SMG (lighter than standard rifle)
- **Ammunition:** 6 magazines (180 rounds total)
- **AT Launcher:** Javelin, SMAW, RPG-7, AT4, etc.
- **AT Ammunition:** 2-3 rockets (depending on system)
- **Equipment:** 1x Smoke Grenade

**Anti-Air (AA) Variant:**
- **Primary Weapon:** Carbine or SMG
- **Ammunition:** 6 magazines (180 rounds total)
- **AA Launcher:** Stinger, Igla, etc.
- **AA Ammunition:** 2-3 missiles
- **Equipment:** 1x Smoke Grenade

- **Bandages:** 2x Field Dressings

# Squad Limitations

- **Maximum 2 per squad** - One AT and one AA, or two AT specialists
- Critical asset for countering armor and air threats
- Must coordinate with Squad Leader for target priority

# Special Abilities

Special Weapons soldiers provide the squad's only organic anti-armor and anti-air capability, making them critical force multipliers:

**Anti-Tank (AT) Specialist:**
- **Vehicle Destruction:** Engage and destroy enemy armor (tanks, IFVs, APCs)
- **Fortification Busting:** Clear bunkers and fortified positions
- **Fire-and-Forget (Javelin):** Top-attack missiles that guide themselves
- **Unguided (RPG/SMAW):** Direct-fire rockets for close-range engagement
- **Building Demolition:** Destroy structures and create breaches

**Anti-Air (AA) Specialist:**
- **Air Denial:** Engage helicopters and low-flying aircraft
- **Heat-Seeking:** Fire-and-forget infrared guided missiles
- **Area Denial:** Force enemy aircraft to avoid area of operations
- **Transport Interdiction:** Prevent enemy air assault and resupply

**Tactical Employment:**
- **Ambush Positions:** Set up kill zones for enemy armor columns
- **Overwatch:** Cover squad advances against vehicle threats
- **Defense Coordination:** Coordinate with Squad Leader on target priority
- **Buddy System:** Often paired with Rifleman assistant for protection

# Advantages

- **Vehicle Lethality:** Only infantry role that can reliably kill heavy armor
- **Air Defense:** Only infantry defense against helicopters and aircraft
- **Strategic Impact:** Single soldier can eliminate multi-million dollar assets
- **Psychological Effect:** Presence deters enemy armor and air use
- **Fortification Breaching:** Can destroy bunkers and structures
- **Long Range (AT):** Some systems effective beyond 2000m (Javelin)

# Disadvantages

- **Severe Weight Penalty:**
  - 25-30% slower movement speed than Rifleman
  - 40% slower sprint speed
  - Launcher system is extremely heavy (15-25kg loaded)
  - Very limited stamina - exhausts rapidly
  - Cannot vault obstacles while carrying launcher
- **Limited Ammunition:**
  - Only 2-3 rockets/missiles total
  - Each shot must count - no room for error
  - Cannot resupply from squad - must return to FOB/supply point
  - May need to drop launcher after expending ammunition
- **Poor Infantry Combat:**
  - Reduced rifle ammunition (6 vs 8-10 mags)
  - Launcher makes you vulnerable in infantry fights
  - Cannot effectively engage in close combat while carrying launcher
  - Must rely on squad for infantry protection
- **Exposure Risk:**
  - Long acquisition and lock-on time for guided missiles (5-10 seconds)
  - Backblast reveals position to enemy
  - Must expose yourself to acquire targets
  - Vulnerable during reload (10-15 seconds)
- **Specialized Role:**
  - Useless without armored or air targets
  - Limited contribution to infantry firefights
  - Cannot use launcher in close quarters (backblast danger)
- **Technical Requirements:**
  - Guided systems require line of sight for lock-on (AA)
  - IR jammers and flares can defeat missiles
  - Some systems have minimum engagement ranges
- **High-Value Target:** Enemy prioritizes killing AT/AA specialists
- **Backblast Zone:** Cannot fire from enclosed spaces (danger to friendly forces)
- **Buddy Dependence:** Needs squad protection to survive and employ weapon effectively
