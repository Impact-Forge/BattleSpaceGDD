# Character Description

The role of the ***Automatic Rifleman*** (AR) is to lay down a high volume of suppressive fire against area targets. He is a vital member of the fire team provides them with a mobile base of rapid fire when assaulting. When used in defensive operations, the Automatic Rifleman can lay down fires equivalent to multiple standard riflemen.\

# Loadout

- **Primary Weapon:** Light Machine Gun (M249 SAW, PKM, RPK, etc.)
- **Ammunition:** 600-800 rounds in belts/drums (3-4 magazines/drums)
  - 200-round belts or 75-100 round drums depending on weapon system
- **Equipment:** 2x Smoke Grenades (no frags due to weight)
- **Special:** Deployable bipod (integrated with weapon)
- **Bandages:** 2x Field Dressings

# Squad Limitations

- **Maximum 1-2 per squad** - Heavy weapons specialist
- Often assigned one Rifleman as assistant to carry extra ammunition

# Special Abilities

The Automatic Rifleman is the fire support backbone of the squad, providing sustained suppressive fire that enables maneuver:

**Suppressive Fire:**
- **High Volume Fire:** Lay down 600-800 rounds of suppressive fire
- **Area Denial:** Suppress enemy positions, forcing them to take cover
- **Support by Fire:** Enable squad advances by pinning enemy forces
- **Defensive Anchor:** Hold defensive positions with sustained fire

**Bipod Deployment:**
- **Deployable Stance:** Use bipod for maximum accuracy and reduced recoil
  - Deploy on ground (prone) or surfaces (cover/windows)
  - Takes 2-3 seconds to deploy/undeploy
  - Significantly improved accuracy and controllability when deployed
  - Can traverse 90-degree arc while deployed
- **Sustained Fire:** Can maintain accurate fire for extended periods when deployed

**Ammunition Management:**
- Receives ammo resupply priority from Riflemen
- Can share ammunition with other Auto-Riflemen of same weapon type

# Advantages

- **Sustained Fire:** 600-800 round capacity allows prolonged engagements
- **Suppression Master:** Most effective role for pinning enemy forces
- **Defensive Power:** Can hold chokepoints and deny areas single-handedly
- **Bipod Accuracy:** When deployed, highly accurate and controllable
- **Squad Support:** Enables squad maneuver through suppressive fire
- **Psychological Effect:** Volume of fire forces enemy to stay in cover

# Disadvantages

- **Heavy Weight Penalty:**
  - 20% slower movement speed than Rifleman
  - 30% slower sprint speed
  - Reduced stamina - exhausts faster when running
  - Cannot vault high obstacles easily
- **Weapon Handling:**
  - Slow ADS (Aim Down Sight) time - 40% slower than rifles
  - Very poor accuracy when firing from hip
  - Severe accuracy penalty when moving/not deployed
  - Long reload time (8-12 seconds for belt/drum change)
- **Bipod Dependency:**
  - Must deploy bipod for effective accurate fire
  - Vulnerable while deploying (2-3 second animation)
  - Limited traverse arc when deployed (90 degrees)
  - Must redeploy to engage new directions
- **Ammunition Dependency:**
  - Burns through ammo quickly in sustained fire
  - Relies on Riflemen for resupply
  - Limited ammo count (3-4 mags) despite high capacity per mag
- **Limited Close Combat:** Poor performance in CQB situations
- **No Grenades:** Cannot carry frags due to weapon/ammo weight
- **Prone Requirement:** Most effective when prone with bipod deployed
