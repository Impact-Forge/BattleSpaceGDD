# Character Description

Players will work as part of a team to operate tanks and assault vehicles and fire weapons to destroy enemy positions.

# Appearance

![8507741418\_ed10dba6ec\_o.jpg](files/8507741418_ed10dba6ec_o.jpg)

# Loadout

- **Primary Weapon:** Compact carbine or submachine gun (vehicle stowage weapon)
- **Ammunition:** 4-6 magazines (120-180 rounds total) - limited due to vehicle focus
- **Equipment:** 1x Smoke Grenade
- **Special Equipment:** Vehicle repair kit, fire extinguisher, crew helmet
- **Bandages:** 2x Field Dressings

# Squad/Team Limitations

- **No squad requirement** - Operates as vehicle crew
- Can join squad or operate independently with vehicle crew
- Multiple crew needed per vehicle (2-4 depending on vehicle type)

# Special Abilities

Crew Members are specialized in operating and maintaining armored vehicles and assault platforms:

**Vehicle Operation:**
- **Tank Operation:** Operate main battle tanks (requires 3-4 crew)
  - Driver, Gunner, Commander, (Loader on some platforms)
  - Coordinate crew positions for maximum effectiveness
- **IFV/APC Operation:** Operate infantry fighting vehicles and armored personnel carriers
  - Driver and Gunner positions (2 crew minimum)
  - Provide fire support and infantry transport
- **Vehicle Combat:** Engage enemy armor, infantry, and fortifications
  - Main gun operation (HEAT, APFSDS, HE rounds)
  - Coaxial and commander's machine guns
  - Missile systems (on equipped platforms)

**Vehicle Maintenance:**
- **Field Repair:** Repair damaged vehicle systems
  - Engine repairs (reduced mobility damage)
  - Track/wheel repairs
  - Turret/weapon system repairs
  - Takes 30-90 seconds depending on damage
- **Fire Suppression:** Extinguish vehicle fires before catastrophic damage
- **Ammunition Management:** Reload vehicle ammunition at supply points
- **Fuel Management:** Refuel vehicles at FOB or fuel trucks

**Tactical Employment:**
- **Combined Arms:** Coordinate with infantry squads for support
- **Overwatch:** Provide fire support for advancing infantry
- **Breakthrough:** Lead assaults against fortified positions
- **Area Denial:** Control key terrain with superior firepower

# Advantages

- **Heavy Firepower:** Access to tank cannons and heavy weapons
- **Armored Protection:** Protected from small arms and shrapnel inside vehicle
- **Mobility:** Fast movement across battlefield in vehicle
- **Force Multiplier:** Single tank worth multiple infantry squads in firepower
- **Multi-Crew Coordination:** Work as team to maximize vehicle effectiveness
- **Repair Capability:** Can repair vehicle damage in field
- **Infantry Support:** Provide critical fire support for ground forces
- **Objective Breakthrough:** Can punch through enemy defenses

# Disadvantages

- **Vehicle Dependency:**
  - Extremely vulnerable outside vehicle (minimal weapons/ammo)
  - Only 120-180 rounds of personal ammunition
  - SMG/carbine ineffective beyond close range
  - Essentially helpless if vehicle destroyed
- **Limited Infantry Combat:**
  - Not trained or equipped for dismounted operations
  - Poor personal weapon and minimal ammunition
  - No grenades or support equipment
  - Must stay with vehicle to be effective
- **Vehicle Vulnerabilities:**
  - Susceptible to AT weapons (RPGs, Javelins, mines)
  - Limited visibility from inside vehicle (blind spots)
  - Can be tracked/immobilized easily
  - Vulnerable to infantry in urban terrain
- **Crew Coordination Required:**
  - Need 2-4 coordinated crew for effectiveness
  - Loss of any crew member reduces capability
  - Communication critical between crew positions
  - Difficult for solo players
- **High-Value Target:**
  - Enemy prioritizes destroying armor
  - AT specialists hunt you specifically
  - Aircraft target vehicles first
  - Mines and IEDs placed on vehicle routes
- **Limited Situational Awareness:**
  - Restricted vision from inside vehicle
  - Hard to see infantry threats (especially AT soldiers)
  - Vulnerable to ambushes in urban/forest terrain
  - Commander must coordinate external awareness
- **Fuel and Ammunition Limits:**
  - Must return to supply points for rearm/refuel
  - Limited ammunition loadout (20-40 main gun rounds)
  - Vehicle useless when out of ammo/fuel
- **Terrain Restrictions:**
  - Cannot operate in dense urban areas effectively
  - Bridges may not support vehicle weight
  - Mud, water, steep slopes limit mobility
  - Easy to get stuck or trapped
- **Catastrophic Loss:**
  - Vehicle destruction often kills entire crew
  - Massive resource loss when vehicle destroyed
  - Long respawn/replacement time for vehicles
- **Infantry Vulnerability:** Exposed when entering/exiting vehicle (easy kills)
- **No Squad Integration:** Operates outside normal squad structure
